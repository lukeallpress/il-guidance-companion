Why feature a district?

The ISBE guidance is deliberately context-sensitive: it asks each district to determine purpose and use locally rather than prescribing one model. That makes a concrete example valuable. New Trier's work shows what "deliberate, context-sensitive, and locally determined" looks like in practice — not as a template to copy, but as one district's honest working-through of the official material.
